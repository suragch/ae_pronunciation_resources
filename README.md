# American English Pronunciation Resources

Audio and video resources for learning the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) and American English pronunciation.

## Contents

- `single_sounds/` — 47 MP3 recordings of individual American English sounds
- `double_sounds/` — 941 MP3 recordings of consonant–vowel / vowel–consonant combinations
- `words/` — 113 MP3 recordings of example words
- `videos/` — 47 MP4 videos demonstrating individual sounds
